## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Graphic Design student at Selçuk University** exploring the intersection of visual design, 3D and technology.

I enjoy turning ideas into things people can **see, interact with and play** — from Blender models and visual identities to Unity games, web experiences and experimental AR projects.

- 🎨 Graphic Design & Visual Identity
- 🧊 3D Modeling with Blender
- 🎮 Game Development with Unity
- 📱 UI/UX & Mobile Experiences
- 🥽 AR / Depth Sensing Experiments
- 🤖 AI-assisted creative workflows

## 🚀 Selected Projects

### 🎮 Number Blast
A mobile number puzzle game being developed in **Unity** for Android, with adventure and classic game modes, progression systems and a custom UI.

`Unity` `C#` `Android` `Game Design` `UI/UX`

### 📱 Galaxy Ultra Scanner
An experimental mobile 3D-scanning project exploring **ARCore depth sensing**, projection diagnostics and real-world spatial data.

`Unity` `ARCore` `C#` `3D` `Android`

### 🎬 [Watchpath](https://github.com/buremiyen/Watchpath)

`TypeScript` `Web` `UI/UX`

`Design` `UI/UX` `Creative Development`
